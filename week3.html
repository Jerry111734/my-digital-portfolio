Weekly reflection · WEEK 3

Web Scraping

Experimenting with methods for collecting information from websites.

What I learned

This workshop introduced web scraping as a way of collecting information from web pages. It showed how information presented on a website can be selected and organised into a dataset for further exploration.

Practical experience

I used Web Scraper (WebScraper.io) to collect information from character pages on the official Marvel website. This gave me a practical opportunity to think about which page elements could be selected and how the collected information could be structured.

My reflection

The activity helped me see that online information does not automatically become useful data. The choices made during collection—such as which pages and fields to include—shape the dataset that is produced. I also need to consider whether the source is appropriate and whether collection respects the website and its users.

What I want to explore

I would like to practise checking the quality and consistency of scraped data, and learn more about how collection choices affect later analysis.
