Workshop Reflections

A record of my learning journey through Digital Practices, exploring digital media, web development and data practices.

WEEK 01

Introducing Digital Practices

This week introduced the module's approach to understanding digital media and the relationship between technology and society.

Learning Focus

Understanding digital practices and exploring social constructionist perspectives on technology.

Personal reflection to be added.

WEEK 02

Creating and Hosting a Website

I explored basic web development by working with HTML and CSS. I also learned how to publish a website using GitHub Pages.

Practical Experience

With guidance, I created web pages and experimented with website structure, styling and online publishing.

Personal reflection to be added.

WEEK 03

Web Scraping

This workshop introduced methods of extracting information from websites.

Practical Experience

I used WebScraper.io to collect information from character pages on the official Marvel website.

Personal reflection and scraping results to be added.

WEEK 04

Data Practices and Data Collection

Our group explored how students use generative AI in arts and humanities education.

Practical Experience

We examined online discussions from platforms including TikTok, Xiaohongshu and Reddit, using data collection tools.

Ethical Considerations

An important challenge was considering users' privacy and ensuring that online content was collected and represented respectfully.

Personal reflection to be added.

WEEK 05

Data and Data Visualisation

This topic focuses on organising, analysing and presenting data through visualisation.

Learning Focus

Exploring how datasets can be structured and transformed into meaningful visual representations.

Practical experience and personal reflection to be added.
