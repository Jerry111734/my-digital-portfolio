Weekly reflection · WEEK 4

Data Practices and Data Collection

Exploring how students use generative AI in arts and humanities education.

What we explored

Our group explored how students use generative AI in arts and humanities education. The activity involved considering online discussions from platforms including TikTok, Xiaohongshu and Reddit, and thinking about how online material might be gathered for research.

Practical experience

We considered the use of data collection tools to examine public online discussions. Comparing different platforms also highlighted that each platform has its own style, audience and conventions, which can influence the kinds of material people post.

Ethical considerations

An important challenge was considering users’ privacy and ensuring that online content was collected and represented respectfully. Publicly accessible information can still involve personal experiences, so research should minimise unnecessary exposure and avoid presenting posts in a misleading way.

My reflection

This workshop helped me recognise that data collection is not a neutral process. Researchers make decisions about sources, keywords, inclusion and interpretation. Those decisions affect whose experiences become visible and how the findings are represented.
