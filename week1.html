Weekly reflection · WEEK 1

Introducing Digital Practices

A reflection on my first week of learning about digital media.

What I learned

This week introduced the module’s approach to understanding digital media and the relationship between technology and society. I began to consider digital media not simply as a collection of tools, but as a set of practices shaped by the people and communities who use them.

Learning focus

An important idea was the social constructionist perspective on technology. This perspective draws attention to the ways social needs, human choices and cultural contexts influence how technologies are developed and used.

My reflection

Before this week, I tended to think of technology mainly in terms of the tools themselves. The workshop encouraged me to look beyond the tools and consider how everyday use, social relationships and cultural expectations also shape digital technology.

What I want to explore

I would like to keep exploring how different people use digital platforms and how those practices influence communication, relationships and everyday experiences.
