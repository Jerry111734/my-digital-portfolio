- `reflection.html` — adds Week 6, 7 and 8 to the reflections index

## How to publish on GitHub

1. Unzip the archive on your computer.
2. In your repository `Jerry111734/my-digital-portfolio`, upload the six HTML files to the **root** of the `main` branch. Choose to replace `index.html`, `reflection.html`, and `week5.html` when asked.
3. Open or create the `assets` folder at the repository root, and upload `week8-group-sensory-map.jpg` to that folder.
4. Commit the changes and reload GitHub Pages. Check the Week 5 → Week 6 → Week 7 → Week 8 links and open the Week 8 image.

**Do not upload the ZIP itself expecting GitHub to unpack it.**
