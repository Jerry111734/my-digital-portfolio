WEEK 01

Introducing Digital Practices

A reflection on my first week of learning about digital media.

What I Learned This Week

This week introduced me to the concept of digital practices and the relationship between technology and society. I began to understand that digital media is not simply about using technological tools, but also about how people interact with and shape technology.

Key Learning

One important idea I explored was the social constructionist perspective on technology. This perspective suggests that technology is influenced by social needs, human choices and cultural contexts.

My Reflection

Before this week, I mainly thought about digital technology as a collection of tools used in everyday life. However, I started to realise that technology and society influence each other. The way people use digital media can also shape communication, relationships and culture.

What I Want to Explore

I would like to explore how digital technologies influence people's everyday experiences and how different users interact with online platforms.

This was my starting point for understanding digital media. I began to realise that technology is not only about tools, but also about how people use them and how they shape society.
