About this portfolio

This portfolio documents my engagement with digital media practices, from understanding how technology and society shape one another to experimenting with web development, data collection and visualisation.

What I am exploring

  • How digital technologies are shaped by social contexts
  • How websites are built and published
  • How online information can be collected responsibly
  • How data can be organised and communicated visually
