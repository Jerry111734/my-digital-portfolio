Weekly reflection · WEEK 5

Data and Data Visualisation

Considering how data can be organised, analysed and communicated visually.

What the topic focused on

This topic focused on organising, analysing and presenting data through visualisation. It introduced the idea that datasets need to be structured and interpreted before they can communicate a meaningful message.

Learning focus

I considered how information can be grouped, compared and transformed into visual representations. Choices such as categories, labels, scales and chart types can influence what an audience notices and how they interpret the data.

My reflection

This topic encouraged me to think about visualisation as more than making data look attractive. A useful visualisation should make the underlying information easier to understand while representing it accurately. It is important to consider what a chart highlights, what it leaves out and whether its design could mislead an audience.

What I want to practise

I would like to gain more experience choosing suitable visual forms for different kinds of data and explaining clearly what a visualisation can—and cannot—show.
